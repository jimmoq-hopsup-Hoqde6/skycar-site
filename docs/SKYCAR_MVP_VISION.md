# Skycar MVP Product Vision

## North star
Skycar is a consumer car-ownership platform. Services acquire the customer; the Garage retains the relationship; membership compounds value as the number of Garage vehicles gives Skycar greater purchasing power with partner brands.

**MVP goal: turn an immediate car problem into a permanent Skycar customer.**

## Flywheel
Problem -> saving -> convenience -> trust -> completed job -> Garage -> repeat services -> membership -> more Garage vehicles -> stronger partner negotiation -> better member value.

## Acquisition wedge
Do not ask a new customer to understand or populate an empty Garage first. Lead with an immediate need:
- scratch or dent repair
- mobile detailing
- mobile cleaning
- other supported mobile car-care services

Initial target hypothesis: private-pay owners with cosmetic/repair work commonly around $450-$1,200, including customers who prefer not to make an insurance claim. Validate this range and savings messaging with Skycar transaction data before public claims.

## Flagship MVP journey
1. Customer chooses the problem/service.
2. For visual damage, camera opens and the customer takes photos.
3. **Skycar analyses** the damage in the background. Do not make "AI inspection" the consumer-facing story.
4. Show an estimated range/guidance where evidence supports it, including likely repair type, expected duration and mobile suitability.
5. CTA: **Want an exact price?**
6. Customer chooses a **Skycar Trusted Mobile Repairer**.
7. For suitable work, fulfilment defaults to **at home** or **at work**. Workshop/panel-shop referral is fallback for work that cannot be safely/effectively completed mobile.
8. Customer receives exact quote/scope, books, tracks the job and pays through Skycar.
9. On completion, prompt: **Add your car to your Skycar Garage.**
10. The Garage is already valuable: before/after photos, invoice, service/repair record, provider, date, warranty/guarantee where applicable and payment record. Never ask the customer to re-enter information Skycar already has.

## Mobile-first supply
Initial network:
- mobile cosmetic/SMART repair technicians
- dent repairers
- mobile detailers
- mobile cleaners
- later, other safe/economical home/work services

Position mobile as the efficient delivery model, not lower-quality work. Emphasise convenience, trust and transparent pricing.

Do not publish absolute claims such as "cheaper than any panel shop" or "50-80% cheaper" until Skycar has evidence. Measure actual comparable savings and later show customers their own cumulative Skycar savings.

## Trust
"Skycar Trusted Repairer" is platform-controlled, never self-assigned. MVP trust signals should cover identity/business verification, capability/category, coverage, availability, transparent scope/quote, verified-job ratings, before/after evidence and a support/dispute path.

## Garage retention
The free Garage is the bridge from a one-off transaction to ongoing ownership:
- repair/service history
- invoices and warranties
- vehicle documents
- reminders
- repeat bookings
- detailing/cleaning
- future mobile maintenance
- relevant benefits/partner offers
- measured Skycar savings

Desired behaviour change: from **"search Google for someone"** to **"open Skycar."**

## Membership and scale
Do not make paid membership the first conversion target. First earn trust and grow Garage density.

Long-term proposition: **Skycar helps make owning your car cheaper and easier.**

As Garage vehicle count grows, negotiate useful benefits with categories such as tyres, roadside assistance, insurance, fuel/charging, car wash/detailing, parts/accessories, servicing, dealerships and finance. Return part of Skycar's purchasing power to members. Benefits should be relevant to the customer's vehicle rather than a generic coupon catalogue.

## MVP success funnel
Measure:
1. visitor -> request started
2. request/photos -> useful estimate/result
3. estimate -> exact-price request
4. exact-price request -> booking
5. booking -> completion
6. completion -> Garage vehicle created/claimed
7. Garage customer -> repeat booking
8. repeat customer -> eventual membership conversion

Also track time to estimate, provider acceptance/completion, average job value, home vs work fulfilment, Garage claim rate and 30/90/180-day repeat rate.

## Scope guardrails
In MVP: service-first entry, photo-led damage request, Skycar analysis, estimated range with guardrails, Trusted Mobile Repairer selection, home/work service, exact quote, booking/status, completion evidence, payment flow, Garage history and the provider workflow needed to fulfil it.

Not MVP: generic workshop-management software, empty-Garage-first acquisition, unsupported nationwide/instant promises, broad insurance claims, every automotive category, membership paywall before value, or unsupported savings guarantees.

## Product test
For every feature ask:

**Does this help turn a real car problem into a long-term Skycar customer?**

If not, it is lower priority than the core funnel.
